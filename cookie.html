Effective Date: 1 April 2024

Hundred Plus ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website https://www.hundredplushr.com (the "Site") to improve your experience and provide relevant content and advertising. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to the owners of the site. Cookies can be "persistent" or "session" cookies. Persistent cookies remain on your device after you close your browser, while session cookies are deleted when you close your browser.

Types of Cookies We Use

  1. Essential Cookies
    These cookies are necessary for the operation of the Site. They enable you to navigate the Site and use its features, such as accessing secure areas.
  2. Performance Cookies
    These cookies collect information about how you use the Site, such as which pages you visit most often. This data helps us improve the Site's performance and provide a better user experience.
  3. Functional Cookies
    These cookies allow the Site to remember choices you make (such as your username, language, or region) and provide enhanced, more personalized features.
  4. Advertising and Targeting Cookies
    These cookies are used to deliver relevant ads to you. They may also limit the number of times you see an advertisement and help measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. They are usually placed by advertising networks with our permission.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Understand and save your preferences for future visits.
  • Compile aggregate data about site traffic and interactions to offer better site experiences and tools in the future.
  • Monitor and analyze the performance, operation, and effectiveness of the Site.
  • Provide personalized content and advertisements based on your browsing behavior.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third parties when you visit the Site. These third parties may include advertising networks and providers of external services like web traffic analysis services. Third-party cookies are set by external domains and are used for the purposes mentioned above.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the option to manage your cookie preferences. You can do this through the cookie settings on the Site or through your web browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to block or delete cookies, but please note that doing so may affect your ability to use certain features of the Site.

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Cookie Policy on the Site. You are advised to review this Cookie Policy periodically for any changes.
